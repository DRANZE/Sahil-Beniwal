What kind of projects do you take on?

Product work with real constraints – SaaS, AI tools, storefronts and the systems behind them. I’m most useful where the problem is messy and the interface still has to ship.

Do you work with startups or larger teams?

Both. Startups when the first version has to be real. Larger teams when a product or agent needs to land inside an existing stack without drama.

How long does a typical project take?

A focused site or agent can ship in weeks. A fuller product – research through deploy – is usually a matter of months.

What does working together look like?

A short call, a written scope, then a tight loop: weekly builds you can click, not slide decks. I stay through launch.

Can you work inside our existing stack?

Yes. Next.js, React, Python, Shopify and common AI tooling are home territory. If your stack is different, I’ll say so before we scope.

How do we get started?

Book a 15-minute call or write with what you’re building and where it’s stuck. If it’s a fit, you get a clear next step.
